About Me

Hello! I'm Colin Leong, a Year 2 Computer Science (Hons) student at Sunway University.

I'm a backend-focused developer with a strong foundation in machine learning and web scraping. I have a strong interest in system design and building scalable systems that turn data into real-world applications.

I enjoy thinking about how different components work together to form reliable and maintainable architectures. I have built RESTful APIs using FastAPI and Flask, structured with clean separation of concerns (routers, services, models, schemas).

On the data side, I have experience building end-to-end pipelines from preprocessing to model evaluation. I'm comfortable developing predictive models for classification, regression and clustering tasks.

I also have practical experience in web scraping, combining browser automation with efficient parsing to build scalable data extraction tools. Through freelance gigs on Upwork, I've assisted clients extract and structure data into clean, reusable CSV datasets.

Currently seeking backend engineering opportunities where I can contribute to robust systems while continuing to deepen my skills in data-driven applications.
