About Me

Hello! I'm Colin Leong, a Year 2 Computer Science (Hons) student at Sunway University.

I'm deeply fascinated by the intelligence behind computers, from Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to the Backend Systems that power modern applications.

Recently, I've been focusing on mastering FastAPI and Flask, exploring best practices for building clean, reliable and scalable backend services.

I also have a strong appreciation for Low-level Programming. I enjoy working with C, as it allows me to understand how software interacts with hardware, uncovering the layers that high-level languages often abstract away.

Above all, I like to learn. Whether it's Data Structures, Computer Architecture or Systems Design, I'm eager to dive deeper and understand how computers really work.
